What’s new in Neon Veins25 updates · 59 changesAll updates
BUILD 61LATESTOct 31, 2026
  • A NEXT line now tells you what to do: under the title menu, at the top of the pause menus and in the open city's objective panel (a perk point to spend, your daily reward, a first Black Market visit, the job boards, the Training Zone if you are new)
  • One-time tips for the first hours: the open city, low health, being spotted and running out of ammo. Settings, Hints and next-step tips, turns them off, and Show every hint again brings them back
  • The Dossier has a new section that says exactly when each hint appears
BUILD 60Oct 28, 2026
  • The story has a new script: the dialogue is rewritten in the cast's own slang, and the Chinese translation follows it line by line
  • Act II-B, the Tidelines: seven new chapters (20A-20G) between Break the Chain and Fire Across Avalon. Rust Harbor's reef crustaceans (Snippy, Pearl, Shelly, Hermit, Pincher and Claw'd, who speaks through CrustAI) against Helix's Cold Engine water pumps: nine operations, six side quests (SQ61-66), three encrypted BUBBLE SIGNAL intercepts and a Tideline Reef entry in the Journal
  • Saves from before this build keep their place. If you are already past chapter 20, the Tidelines wait in Chapter Select
  • Kitchens, decoding rooms and tribunals in the story now play in indoor spaces
BUILD 59Oct 24, 2026
  • The open city now has day and night and weather: clear, overcast, rain, storms with lightning, and fog. They change what guards see, how many people are out and how cars grip (Settings can turn it off)
  • Side jobs at the cyan job boards: courier runs, bounties, hostage rescues, car theft, data heists and contract hits, in three tiers
  • Challenges on the title menu: a daily and a weekly operation, the same for everyone, with medals, a daily streak and leaderboards (daily, weekly, best drift) for signed-in players. Credits moved to Settings
  • 8 new achievements (62 in all); the Dossier explains the weather, the jobs and the challenges

BUILD 58Oct 24, 2026
  • Achievements: 54 of them, each paying credits, and a lifetime statistics page (title menu and pause menu)
  • Three save slots: Save Slots on the title menu, and New Game asks which slot to use
  • New Game+: after an ending, start again with your gear, cyberdeck, implants and perks; the enemies get tougher each time
  • Key rebinding in Settings, Controls, with toggle sprint and sneak
  • Accessibility: interface size, subtitle size and backdrop, and a colour-blind filter
BUILD 57Oct 22, 2026
  • The little continue arrow is back at the end of each dialogue line
BUILD 56Oct 22, 2026
  • Dialogue is now one clean subtitle line everywhere: the old boxed dialogue panel with a portrait and the blinking arrow is gone
BUILD 55Oct 21, 2026
  • New: Rewards on the title menu. Claim a daily reward (a seven-day streak of credits, parts, lockpicks, grenades, medical patches and XP) and type promo codes for one-off gifts
BUILD 54Oct 20, 2026
  • Removed the 2098 text and rules under the NEON VEINS logo on the title screen
BUILD 53Oct 20, 2026
  • The title logo is smaller and centred on the menu panel, so it sticks out of both sides of it
BUILD 52Oct 20, 2026
  • New NEON VEINS logo in the style of a cyberpunk game poster: heavy angular yellow lettering cut by slashes, a cyan glitch fringe with sliding glitch bars, and a wide-spaced 2098 underneath; still on one row and breaking out of the menu box
  • The same logo now shows on the splash, language and credits screens
BUILD 51Oct 18, 2026
  • Enemies and people no longer vanish, lose body parts or turn to blobs at range on the lower graphics presets
BUILD 50Oct 18, 2026
  • New NEON VEINS logo: one row, drawn as cyberpunk neon-tube lettering that breaks out of the menu panel and runs over the tower
BUILD 49Oct 17, 2026
  • Jumping: Space now jumps (and hops onto crates and low cover); dash moved to Alt
  • Reloads redone: the gun comes to the centre of the screen so the magazine change is easy to see
  • Fixed the random screen blackouts and made quality changes smoother, with a lighter Ultra preset
  • Smoother, rounder hands and fingers in first person
  • New Crosshair & cursor settings: six crosshair styles, colours, size, outline, and a custom menu pointer
BUILD 48Oct 17, 2026
  • Sound overhaul: every sound is new, with gunfire, footsteps and explosions placed in the world (distance, muffling behind walls, left/right)
  • New ambience for the city, rooftops, apartment and every interior; heartbeat at low health, alarm siren, win and fail stingers
  • Richer music with a kick-pumped pad and a lead melody; new engine and tyre sounds
BUILD 47Oct 17, 2026
  • Dialogue choices restyled: red skill tag, key chip, yellow selected bar and cyan options
BUILD 46Oct 17, 2026
  • Clothes: slimmer sleeves, a proper trouser fork, lighter trousers and boots, softer folds
  • Hands: warmer skin, knuckles and nails
BUILD 45Oct 17, 2026
  • Faces are blank again: a smooth, featureless head with no eyes, brows or face texture
  • Hands, hair, hood and collar from the last build stay
BUILD 44Oct 16, 2026
  • Characters rebuilt around a real human head: proper eyes with an iris and pupil, brows, lashes, lips, ears and a neck
  • Realistic relaxed hands that come out of the sleeves, and fitted hair, hood and braids
  • Pedestrians now have eyes too
BUILD 43Oct 16, 2026
  • Faces are left blank (plain smooth skin, no painted eyes, nose or mouth) instead of the uncanny painted faces; Ghost's courier hood is now a proper draped hood instead of a cap with a brim
  • New human hands with separate fingers and a thumb, bare skin for everyone except soldiers; narrower hips and legs
  • Hair has more strand detail and soft ends, long hair now falls clear of the face
BUILD 42Oct 16, 2026
  • Faces and clothes look much more natural: reworked head proportions (eyes, nose, mouth and chin in the right places, a firmer jaw), calmer matte skin, softer hairlines and better hair shapes
  • Clothes are real garments now: a zip jacket with pockets, collar, seams and a ribbed hem, woven fabric on sleeves and trousers, leather boots and gloves, and narrower shoulders so heads are the right size
  • Cybernetic eyes now glow from the eye itself instead of a flat glowing slab
BUILD 41Oct 16, 2026
  • Realistic people: enemies, civilians, pedestrians, story characters, Ghost and other players now have smooth human bodies, a sculpted face with painted eyes, brows, lips and skin pores, real hair and proper jackets instead of the old low-poly blocks
  • Ghost's look drives the 3D body: skin tone, hair style and colour, jacket, visor, shades, glowing optics and cyberware all show on the model
  • The character creator now shows a live 3D preview of Ghost that you can drag to turn
BUILD 40Oct 15, 2026
  • Graphics now start in WebGL, which draws every level correctly; this fixes the black 3D view (only the HUD showing) in the apartment and the city on some computers
  • WebGPU is optional now: add #webgpu to the address to try it (add #gl to go back); a red notice appears if it misbehaves
BUILD 39Oct 15, 2026
  • Fixed a black 3D view (only the HUD showing) in browsers that use WebGPU
BUILD 38Oct 13, 2026
  • Cars rebuilt: real grip and sliding, so you can drift with the handbrake or by cornering hard
  • Drift scoring pays credits and a mini-turbo (combo up to x5)
  • Coupe, sedan and van bodies with turning wheels, brake lights, five gears, tyre smoke and skid marks
  • Cars take damage: smoke and sparks, and a full-speed wall still blows them up
BUILD 37Oct 13, 2026
  • Melee weapons now have a cooldown: at least 0.6 s between swings, shown as a time and a filling bar on the weapon panel
  • The Training Zone no longer shows a minimap
