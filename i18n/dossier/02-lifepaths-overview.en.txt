# THE THREE LIFEPATHS: AN OVERVIEW

New Avalon has more kinds of people than any list can hold, but the Bureau's own records sort the living into three broad piles, and so does the Suite. A lifepath is not a job and not a class. It is the place your body learned to be afraid in. Each of the three below has a home district, a way of earning a living, a handful of habits that never went away and a debt to the city that the city has not forgotten.

The Lantern Row Kid is the street. You grew up in the narrow lanes under the paper lanterns, where every stranger is a favour waiting to be called in. You are the one who knows what things cost, who patched up the friend nobody else would touch, and who always has a little more in your pocket than the clerk thinks. The Helix Contractor is the corporation. You spent years inside the tower as a badge on a lanyard, invisible in the way the company liked, and you learned how security rooms think, how networks are guarded and how to look like you belong. The Glass Wastes Courier is the road. You carried cargo across the long dead stretch between the last transit stack and the first city light, on rails and on foot and sometimes on nothing at all, and you are faster and more reckless than anyone who has only ever known walls.

Every lifepath in this document is described the same way, so that you can compare them side by side. First comes who you were, which is the story the Suite will tell your friends. Then how it shaped you, which explains why the numbers are what they are. Then the numbers themselves, in full, with every multiplier written out and an example of what it feels like in an operation. Then the cost: every lifepath has a drawback, and the drawbacks are real. Then how the city treats you, which covers your starting standing with the five forces whose trust you will spend the whole story trying to keep, and finally some advice on playing the lifepath well and spending its free attribute points sensibly.

Three more things hold for all of them. Each lifepath sets minimum levels in two attributes; you can raise those attributes but never lower them, and the minimums are included in every figure quoted under "at the start" below. Each lifepath leaves you four free attribute points to place wherever you like, up to a maximum level of eight in any single attribute. And each lifepath begins your story with different standings in the city's five ledgers: Raven Trust, Voss Integrity, Civilian Hope, Corporate Leverage and AI Sympathy. These are the numbers the story reads when a character decides whether to open a door for you, so they matter even if no weapon ever fires.

If you like to choose by temperament, here is the short version. Choose the Lantern Row Kid if you want to be hard to kill slowly and expensive to ignore: you heal, you earn, you recover from mistakes. Choose the Helix Contractor if you want to avoid the fight altogether: you are seen late, you hack quickly and you pay for it in a thinner body and a louder reputation. Choose the Glass Wastes Courier if you want the fight to be about movement: you are fast, you dodge often, your hands are steady and you never learned to sneak.
