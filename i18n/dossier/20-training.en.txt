# THE TRAINING ZONE

## What it is

The Training Zone is a practice facility opened from the title menu. It uses a temporary character, so it never reads or changes your save: your credits, parts, perks and progress stay exactly as they were, and nothing you do in there is kept. Every weapon in the facility is yellow and black practice gear wearing the TRAINING ZONE logo. You carry one weapon at a time and there is no hotbar: the facility issues you the weapon for the part you are in (see Weapons below), and it also lends you every attachment, implant and vest, a full cyberdeck and plenty of credits and lockpicks.

## The six rooms

The rooms form a loop, and a step list at the top of the screen walks you through it in nineteen steps. Movement: cross the hall, then dash. Armory: press E at the ARMORY board to fit an attachment to your weapon, then close it. Range: knock down three targets, reload, then fight the live-fire drills. Stealth: knock out the dummy from behind (a short hold keeps it down briefly, a long hold keeps it down longer, a hold that is too long kills it) or with a few stun shots, zip-tie it and hide it, then stab a second dummy from behind with the knife. Netrunning: hack three terminals, each of a different kind, use PING and SCAN, then try your cyberweapon on the dummies. Security: break the camera or the breaker box, pick the locked cache, disable the alarm panel and crawl through the vent to the exit hall, where the graduation exam waits. Step into the green beam when all nineteen steps are done.

## Weapons

Each part of the zone issues you its own weapon, and you keep it until the next part. The movement hall, the first range steps and the security room give you the Courier Sidearm, which is accurate and steady. The live-fire drills change weapon every round: the sidearm in round 1, the Vector SMG in round 2 (rapid fire at close and middle range), the Boarding Shotgun in round 3 (devastating up close), the Nightglass Rifle in round 4 (one powerful long-range shot; right click zooms and SHIFT steadies the scope) and the Civic Stability Rifle in round 5. In the stealth room you carry the Helix Stun Pistol, a nonlethal weapon: a few stun shots knock a guard out instead of killing them. Once the body is hidden you are issued Ghost's Monoblade, a knife: it is silent, and a blow to a guard who has not noticed you drops them at once, so crouch and approach from behind. In the netrunning room you carry the Living Cipher cyberweapon, which costs RAM and stuns the target and the others near it. For the graduation exam you carry the Ghostbreaker Minigun: hold the trigger to spin the barrels up.

## Dummies

Dummies are guards that never shoot, take a great deal of damage and stand up again a few seconds after they go down, as long as you are more than four metres away (fourteen seconds if you hid the body). Nothing a dummy does can hurt you.

## Live fire

The live-fire drills are five rounds in the range, and the bots in them shoot back. Round 1 is two bots that stand still and miss a lot, and they deal 30 percent of normal damage. Round 2 is three bots that move between cover (50 percent). Round 3 adds a heavy gunner (70 percent). Round 4 is a riot shield, a sniper and a drone (85 percent): shoot the shield bot in the back, and break line of sight when the sniper's red laser appears. Round 5 is everything at full strength, with grenades and a rusher. Every round, the bots fire faster and aim better than in the one before, they always know where you are, and their visors glow yellow so you can tell them from the dummies. You cannot die in here: if you go down, you stand up again with full health and the round starts over, a little easier each time, and after three falls in one round the instructor waves you through. Clearing a round restores your health and armor.

## The graduation exam

The last hall holds the Instructor, the training zone's boss, and you cannot leave until you have beaten it. It is the same fight as a story boss, at reduced strength. The Instructor wears a shield: override both pylons (E, a breach hack each, and each costs RAM) to drop it for eleven seconds, and shoot the Instructor while it is down, because the shield reboots and the pylons lock again. It has three phases. In phase one it fires bursts and rings of slow shots. In phase two it also fires a spiral and calls in two drones. In phase three it fires faster and charges at you, so dash (SPACE) through the attacks. Its shots deal 70 percent of a story boss's damage and it has 85 percent of the health. If you go down, you stand up again with full health, the Instructor is stunned for three seconds, and its damage drops by 20 percent for every fall (never below 30 percent). Beating it restores your health and opens the green beam.
