# EVERY DOOR IN NEW AVALON: A NOTE ON GOING INSIDE

## Doors

In the open city every building has a door: a glowing frame on a street-facing side, 1,133 of them in all (996 ordinary buildings and the 137 landmarks that have a solid footprint). Walk up to one and press E. The street is put away and the building's own interior is built. When you leave through the glowing door inside, the street is rebuilt and you are standing where you were, facing away from the door. Doors are not offered in co-op.

## What is behind them

A building is the same place every time you enter it. What it is depends on its district and its size: apartments and noodle bars where the heights and the hive crowd together, clinics and clubs in the Memory District, offices and server rooms under Helix, warehouses and workshops along the harbour and the rail yards. The named landmarks keep their names, and a hall, a cathedral, a vault, a dome, a factory or a clinic is laid out as one. The inside is always bigger than the outside: between seven and seventeen four-metre cells wide, with up to four rooms joined by doorways, and every cell of the floor can be reached from the door.

## What you find

There are people to talk to, one to three in each building, and they each have a few things to say. There are one or two stashes, and a stash is searched once per save: it pays between 20 and 90 credits, plus a quarter more for each act you have reached. Nobody inside is hostile, and you cannot be hunted there; the way out is always the door you came in by.
